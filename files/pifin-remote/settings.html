Welcome to PiFin

Your Jellyfin server's address, as you open it in a browser.

Tailscale (optional)

To reach your server from anywhere: paste an auth key from the Tailscale admin console. Skip if your server is on this network.

Setup hotspot password

When no known Wi-Fi is around, PiFin opens its setup hotspot so a phone can pick a network. Choose its password (8 to 63 characters), or leave it empty to keep the one the TV shows.

PIN (optional)

A PIN protects the server address, Tailscale, the hotspot password and factory reset. Leave empty for none.

Almost there

Now sign in on the TV: it shows a Quick Connect code. In the Jellyfin app on this phone, open Settings > Quick Connect and enter the code.
